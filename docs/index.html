PuraPDF+ Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

PuraPDF+ ("the app", "we", "our") is a PDF toolkit for iOS and Android. This page explains what happens to your data when you use it.

The short version

Every PDF you open, edit, merge, split, compress, sign, or scan is processed entirely on your device. We do not have servers that receive, store, or process your documents — we never see them, and we cannot see them. The only data leaving your device is what our advertising partner (Google AdMob, described below) collects to show ads.

Documents and files

  • PDF files you open and any files the app generates (merged, split, compressed, edited, watermarked, signed, or scanned PDFs) are read, processed, and saved entirely on your device.
  • The app never uploads your documents anywhere.
  • Generated files are listed in the app's Recents screen, which reads them directly from your device's storage. You can delete any file, or clear all of them, at any time from Recents — this deletes the file from your device.

Camera and photo library

  • The Document Scanner feature uses your camera to capture pages. The photos it takes are used only to build a PDF on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.
  • The Image ⇄ PDF feature can import photos from your library to convert them to a PDF, or export PDF pages as images to your library. This happens entirely on-device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

PuraPDF+ shows ads through Google AdMob to support free use of the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and app usage data to select and measure ads, and may use this data across other apps and services per Google's own policies. We do not control what AdMob collects — see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Ad Policy for details.

  • EEA / UK / Switzerland: the app asks for your consent (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before requesting personalized ads, as required by GDPR/UK data protection law.
  • iOS: the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before requesting ads that use tracking, as required by Apple.

What we don't do

  • We don't require an account, login, or any personal information to use the app.
  • We don't run analytics or crash-reporting services that collect your documents or personal data.
  • We don't sell your data — we don't have your data to sell.

Children's privacy

PuraPDF+ is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
